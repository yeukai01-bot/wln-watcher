WHY 1,000

Nobody should run a care service on their own

Most care leaders carry the hard calls alone: the 6am sickness call, the safeguarding decision, the night before an inspection. I did it for fifteen years as a CQC Registered Manager, and the thing that made the biggest difference was never a template. It was having people to ask.

The Well-Led Network exists so that every registered manager, owner and nominated individual in the UK has that. A thousand care leaders who share what works is a thousand services that are safer, better led and better prepared. That is the mission, and you can help build it.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining the campaign is free, and so is joining the Well-Led Network community.

What counts as a successful referral?

Someone who signs up through your link and then joins the Well-Led Network community with their own email address. They must be new to the community and work in or around UK care.

I am already a member. Can I take part?

Yes. Get your link and share it. Your rewards come from the new people you introduce.

How do I receive my rewards?

By email, automatically, as soon as a milestone is reached. Paid resources come with a personal one-time code that you use on the Well-Led Network.

What is the Well-Led Live VIP Upgrade?

VIP access to Well-Led Live 2027, our online summit for UK care leaders on 18 to 20 March 2027, 5pm to 8pm UK time, including the VIP implementation workshop and group Q&A. Claim it before the event starts. If the event has already passed when you reach 5 referrals, you will be offered an equivalent alternative.

Do I have to give you my colleagues' details?

No, and please don't. You share your own link with people you know. They decide whether to sign up.

How do I see my progress?

Come back to this page with the same email address and your dashboard shows your referrals and your next reward. We also email you as you reach each milestone.
