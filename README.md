# Well-Led Network Morning Watcher and Reply Service

Every morning this job reads official sources, picks the new developments worth an article, drafts the best one in Yeukai's voice, fact-checks it against the source, and sends everything to Yeukai on Telegram. **It never publishes anything by itself.** Yeukai approves by replying in the same Telegram chat, and a scheduled Claude task publishes only what he approved.

## What it watches

- GOV.UK feeds: Care Quality Commission, Department of Health and Social Care, adult social care, care worker visas and sponsorship, immigration rules changes, the Employment Rights Act, deprivation of liberty, and Oliver McGowan training
- New legislation on legislation.gov.uk, filtered to care-relevant law
- CQC news
- Skills for Care news
- Acas Employment Rights Act pages
- Optional: a Google Sheet (published as CSV) of questions from Facebook and LinkedIn groups, with the columns `date, group, question, link`. The job never logs into Facebook or LinkedIn itself.

## What arrives on Telegram each morning

1. **Recommended topics.** Up to 5, best first. Each has:
   - a headline written in the style that performs
   - why it matters now
   - which of Yeukai's offers it leads towards
   - a score out of 25 (urgency multiplied by client pull)
   - any key date
   - an idea for a free tool to go with it
   - the source link
2. **Already covered.** New developments on subjects with an existing article, so that article can be updated instead.
3. **Set aside for you.** Anything political or contested. These are listed, never drafted.
4. **Sources that failed.** Any source that could not be read, so nothing fails silently.
5. **A full draft of the top pick,** as a Markdown file. It has already been through a second fact-check pass: every claim not supported by the source is removed or marked [CHECK], dashes used as punctuation are removed, and anything controversial is flagged.

## Replying on Telegram

Reply in the same chat the morning list arrives in:

| You type | What happens |
|---|---|
| `1 3` | Topics 1 and 3 are approved. Any topic without a draft is drafted and fact-checked straight away, and the draft is sent to you. |
| `queue` | Shows what is approved and waiting to be published. |
| `cancel 2` | Withdraws topic 2 before it goes live. |
| `write <link>` | Asks for an article on any page you've found, for example a CQC news item. You can add a note after the link. It is approved and drafted straight away. |
| `help` | Shows these instructions. |

- **Who can use it:** only messages from your own chat are accepted.
- **Publishing:** a scheduled Claude task reads the approved topics at a set time and builds the free download. It then publishes the article on welllednetwork.com with a cover image, creates the Shorby link and reports back. You then get a "Live:" message with both links.
- **Chrome must be open:** publishing uses Claude in Chrome, so your computer needs to be on with Chrome open at that time.

**Use a dedicated Telegram bot for this.** The reply service takes over the bot's incoming messages, so a bot that already runs another automation should not be reused. Create a new one in BotFather (for example "Well-Led Topics").

## Voice and safety rules

These live in `voice_brief.md`, the same brief used for the September 2026 articles:

- British English, first person, calm authority
- Facts from primary sources only, no invented anecdotes
- Nothing controversial
- The free-download block near the top
- The Ask Questions and free strategy call invitations at the end

## Setup on Render

1. Put this folder in a GitHub repository that Render can see.
2. In Render, choose **New, then Blueprint**, pick the repository, and it will read `render.yaml`. That file creates:
   - the cron job `wln-morning-watcher`, which runs daily at 05:00 UTC (06:00 UK summer time, 05:00 winter)
   - the free web service `wln-replies`, which receives your Telegram replies
   - a free memory store, which remembers what was reported and what you approved
3. Paste these secrets into Render. They never live in the code:
   - `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`: from console.anthropic.com
   - `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`: from BotFather, for the bot that sends your morning briefing
   - `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID`: your chat with that bot
   - `QUESTIONS_CSV_URL`: optional

   `WEBHOOK_SECRET` and `APPROVALS_KEY` are generated by Render automatically.
4. Once the web service is live, send `help` to the bot to check replies work. Then open the cron job and click **Trigger Run** to get the first morning list.

## Settings you can change in Render

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `LOOKBACK_HOURS` | 48 | How far back to look for new items |
| `MAX_TOPICS` | 5 | Topics in the morning list |
| `MAX_DRAFTS` | 1 | Full drafts written each morning |
| `MIN_SCORE` | 12 | Minimum score out of 25 for a topic to be recommended |
| `ANTHROPIC_MODEL` | claude-sonnet-4-5 | The Claude model to use |
| `DRY_RUN` | unset | Set to 1 to send the list without writing drafts |

After each article goes live, add its title to `existing_articles.txt` so it is not proposed again.

## Cost

- The Render cron job starter plan is a few dollars a month (usage based).
- The reply web service and the memory store are free. The free web service sleeps when idle, so the first reply after a quiet spell can take up to a minute to answer.
- Claude usage is roughly one triage call plus one draft and one fact-check per morning.

## Test

`python test_run.py` runs the whole pipeline and the reply service offline, with fake sources and a fake Claude. It checks:

- the morning list is built correctly
- controversial items are set aside
- a broken source does not stop the run
- old items are skipped
- the same story from two sources appears once
- a second run reports nothing new
- approving, queueing, cancelling and publishing through replies all work
- replies from strangers are ignored

## CQC report radar (client outreach)

After the topics, the same morning job searches the CQC website ("published in the last week", around 40 towns and cities across England, 25-mile radius) for services rated Inadequate or Requires improvement, then keeps only adult social care services using the CQC API. The API alone is not enough: it does not yet carry the new-style assessment ratings. For each one it finds the published contact email on the service's own website, checks the provider's company number, and drafts a short, kind email offering a free 30-minute call plus the matching free article. The first run looks back a month.

What arrives on Telegram: a summary, then one message per service (P1, P2 ...) with the full draft. Nothing is ever sent automatically:

- Tap **Open email** under a draft. Your mail app opens with the address, subject and message already filled in. Read it and press Send.
- `sent P1 P3`: record that you sent them.
- `leads`: today's list and what has been sent.
- `remove name@example.com`: when someone replies "remove", send this and the radar will never draft to them again.

UK law (PECR): unsolicited emails are only drafted to send to limited companies. Sole traders and partnerships are marked **call or write**. Every email carries an opt-out line.

Setup: register free at api-portal.service.cqc.org.uk, copy your primary key, and add it to the `wln-secrets` environment group in Render as `CQC_API_KEY`.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `RADAR_MAX_PER_DAY` | 10 | Most prospects per morning |
| `RADAR_SEARCH_PLACES` | about 40 towns | Places searched, comma separated |
| `RADAR_PRIORITY_REGIONS` | South East,London | Listed first |
| `RADAR_ONLY_PRIORITY_REGIONS` | unset | Set to 1 to ignore other regions |
